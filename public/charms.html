Wayward Compass

Dirtmouth

  • Bought from Iselda for 220 Geo after the first encounter with Cornifer.
  • "Whispers its location to the bearer whenever a map is open, allowing wanderers to pinpoint their current location."
  • It serves as a sort of walking stick for players who are still getting used to the map.

This charm is very useful for exploration and is good for newbies doing their first playthroughs. It marks the player on the map whenever they open it, allowing them to see exactly where they are. Once a player gets to fully know the layout of the map, it is recommended for them to switch to other charms that can be more useful in combat situations.

Gathering Swarm

Dirtmouth

  • Bought from Sly for 300 Geo.
  • "Useful for those who can't bear to leave anything behind, no matter how insignificant."
  • Primarily to make grinding Geo easier.

This charm spawns a swarm that collects any dropped geo near the player. It can collect Geo that has fallen into spikes, thorns, and hard-to-reach places. Though, it is important to note that Gathering Swarm does not collect Geo that falls into acid, Geo dropped by the Grubfather, or Geo rewarded from completing a Colosseum of the Fools trial.

Stalwart Shell

Dirtmouth

  • Bought from Sly for 200 Geo.
  • "Builds resilience. When recovering from damage, the bearer will remain invulnerable for longer."
  • Tank type, made for hard to dodge attacks

It is useful primarily during boss battles, where it is more difficult to dodge attacks or for newer players who are still unused to the parrying and dodging mechanics of the game. The long invulnerability time (about 1.7s) makes it easier to overwhelm bosses with high damage or heal quickly, while the shorter recoil time (0.8s) allows the player to bounce back from attacks faster.

Soul Catcher

Ancestral Mound

  • Found at the very end of the Ancestral Mound, after killing the Elder Baldur.
  • "Used by shamans to draw more SOUL from the world around them."
  • One of the earliest charms available in the game.

This charm increases the amount of Soul gained by the player when hitting enemies with their Nail. The effect is small, but it remains useful for a good amount of time for healing purposes, especially for players who are still getting used to the game mechanics and still get hit a lot.

Shaman Stone

Forgotten Crossroads

  • Bought from Salubra for 220 Geo.
  • "Said to contain the knowledge of past generations of shaman."
  • Incredibly useful for speedrunning.

This charm increases the damage of Spells and the size of Vengeful Spirit or Shade Soul. It increases the damage by 33-51% depending on the spell. It is incredibly powerful, as it can make spells one-shot many enemies, and it makes boss fights easier by allowing the player to safely deal more damage from a distance.

Soul Eater

Resting Grounds

  • [REQUIRES DESOLATE DIVE/DESCENDING DARK UNLOCKED] Use Desolate Dive/Descending Dark on the grave just under the Stag Station then head east in the passageway. The Charm is found next to the sarcophagus of a Snail Shaman.
  • "Forgotten shaman artifact, used to draw SOUL from still-living creatures."
  • A stronger Soul Catcher.

This charm greatly increases the amount of Soul gained by the player when hitting enemies with their Nail. It is basically a stronger version of the Soul Catcher.

Dashmaster

Fungal Wastes

  • Found beneath a statue below the Mantis Village in the Fungal Wastes, near the entrance to the Royal Waterways.
  • "Bears the likeness of an eccentric bug known only as 'The Dashmaster'."
  • Useful for early-game traversing.

This charm decreases the cooldown of the Mothwing Cloak and allows the player to dash downwards. The ability to dash downwards is useful in certain platforming segments or in dodging certain attacks.

Sprintmaster

Dirtmouth

  • Bought from Sly after acquiring the Shopkeeper's Key for 400 Geo.
  • "Bears the likeness of a strange bug known only as 'The Sprintmaster'"
  • Useful when fighting agile bosses that like to move around a lot.

This charm increases the run speed of the player by around 20%. This speed buff is only applied when the player is grounded and not in walk-zones or in liquid such as acid or water.

Grubsong

Forgotten Crossroads

  • Gifted by the Grubfather after the player has freed 10 Grubs.
  • "Contains the gratitude of freed grubs."
  • Provides a decent amount of Soul to use as healing in compensation for taking damage.

This charm grants the player 15 soul when taking damage. It does not provide enough Soul per hit to grant a full heal on its own, but when combined with other ways to gain Soul, this charm's effectiveness increases significantly.

Grubberfly's Elegy

Forgotten Crossroads

  • Gifted by the Grubfather after the player has freed all 46 Grubs.
  • "Contains the gratitude of grubs who will move to the next stage of their lives. Imbues weapons with a holy strength."
  • Powerful as a crowd-control tool, and useful for fighting bosses that have multiple enemies fighting the player at once.

This charm causes Nail hits to fire a projectile which deals half of the Nail's damage per hit. These projectiles can pierce enemies infintely. These buffs are only active when the player has full health.

Fragile Heart

Fungal Wastes

  • Purchased from Leg Eater for 350 Geo. If the player has the Defender's Crest equipped, Leg Eater lowers the price to 280 Geo.
  • "Increases the health of the bearer, allowing them to take more damage."
  • Quite a potent charm.

This charm increases the player's health by 2 masks. However, if the player dies while the charm is equipped, it will break and the player will have to pay the Leg Eater 200 Geo to repair it. (160 Geo if the Defender's Crest is equipped)

Fragile Greed

Fungal Wastes

  • Purchased from Leg Eater for 250 Geo. If the player has the Defender's Crest equipped, Leg Eater lowers the price to 200 Geo.
  • "Causes the bearer to find more Geo when defeating enemies."
  • Recommended to only use in situations where death is unlikely.

This charm increases the amount of Geo that is dropped by enemies. However, if the player dies while the charm is equipped, it will break and the player will have to pay the Leg Eater 150 Geo to repair it. (120 Geo if the Defender's Crest is equipped)
